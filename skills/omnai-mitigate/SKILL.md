# OmnAI Mitigate

1. Use for `incident-response` and `release-failure` before normal diagnosis.
2. Choose the smallest reversible action that reduces customer or operational impact.
3. Preserve logs, traces, versions, timestamps, and other evidence before destructive actions.
4. Record action, reversibility, side effects, remaining impact, owner, and evidence in `research.md`.
5. Mitigation is not root-cause completion; continue through research/debug/fix.
